Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Purpose

Hermes Calendar is a personal-use integration for its owner. It accesses Google Calendar only to carry out calendar actions requested by the owner.

Data accessed

The integration may access Google Calendar event information, including event titles, times, descriptions, locations, attendees, and calendar identifiers, only as needed to read, create, update, or delete events requested by the owner.

Use and sharing

Calendar data is used only to provide the requested calendar action. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Storage and security

Google authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer at ~/.hermes/google_token.json. The integration communicates with Google APIs only to perform owner-requested actions.

Retention and deletion

Calendar data is retained only as required for the requested action. The owner can revoke authorization from the Google Account security settings or remove the local authorization token.
